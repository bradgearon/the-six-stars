## Production notes

The strongest recurring enemy families from this brainstorm are:

- false-holy Virethorn Saints and slime saints;
- alien cave owls and owl hosts;
- Bone Bloom relatives and marrow creatures;
- Leviathan tonguefish, door-eels, and scale wraiths;
- Oculthorax eye-beasts and apostle variants;
- void/slime parasites and dream-eater gels;
- blood/root/choir plant horrors.
