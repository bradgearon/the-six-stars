## Role

## Core conflict

Cannot let go; confuses love with ownership; wants to make dark angels bleed for what they did.

## Relationship and romance flavor

Darkly passionate, jealous, intense, loyal when healed, needs careful boundaries.

## Angel story

### Dark angel story — Worms in the Veins

Maera tells the Last Cat that a dark form came to her in the blood caves as a big black beast, told her blood proved she was cursed, and made her feel worms crawling and biting inside her veins. He made her watch herself bleed out in visions and called it mercy.

**Gameplay use:** This story explains Maera's rage and possession wound. The wise path redirects her need to make the dark angels bleed into protection, justice, and building something good from rot.

Full story: [`docs/dialogue/star-angel-stories.md`](../../dialogue/star-angel-stories.md).
