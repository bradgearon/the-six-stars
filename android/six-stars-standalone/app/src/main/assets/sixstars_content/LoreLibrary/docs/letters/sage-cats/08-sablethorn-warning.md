## Letter Text

To Behemoth and every great creature,

The hands holding your heavens are skilled in your destruction.

Without reason and without remorse, they will rise against you from deep within the Great Bosom.

Do not mistake patience for mercy.

The furnace of the void burns ever brightly, and it is like a sun compared to the work of our hands.

You believe your enemies distant.

They are already beneath your feet.

Your enemy's Captain and Commander,

Sage Sablethorn
