# Alien Flora Catalog

1. Prismroot Lily

Elements: Light + Water
A glowing cave lily with glass petals and roots full of blue liquid light. It opens when the player gets close and refracts beams across the room.

Gameplay: rotating laser hazard / puzzle plant.

2. Virethorn Widowvine

Elements: Virethorn + Shadow
A hanging black-green vine that looks like dead rope until it drops. Its leaves are shaped like little hooked knives.

Gameplay: ambush plant that grabs the player from ceilings.

3. Bloodcup Lantern

Elements: Blood + Light
A red flower with a glowing cup-shaped center. It looks like a save-point flower from far away, but its light pulses like a heartbeat.

Gameplay: lures the player close, then spits tracking blood sparks.

4. Void Orchid Maw

Elements: Void + Virethorn
A purple-black orchid with a tiny black hole in the center of its bloom. Its petals curl inward like teeth.

Gameplay: pulls the player toward thorn pits or enemies.

5. Ashpetal Cactus

Elements: Fire + Shadow
A black cactus with burning orange cracks. Its flowers are made of ash and ember.

Gameplay: fires ember needles in bursts; explodes if hit with fire.

6. Slimeglass Fern

Elements: Slime + Light + Water
A translucent fern made of jelly and crystal veins. It slowly waves even when there is no wind.

Gameplay: slippery platform plant; can also heal slime enemies nearby.

7. Bone Bloom Sproutling

Elements: Bone Bloom + Blood
A small walking plant with a skull-shaped bud and red root-legs. It is like a baby cousin of Bone Bloom.

Gameplay: charges, then plants itself and grows bone spikes.

8. Star-Eater Moss

Elements: Void + Light
A patch of black moss covered in tiny star-specks. When light touches it, the stars vanish one by one.

Gameplay: absorbs light projectiles and darkens the room.

9. Weeping Halo Reed

Elements: Water + Light + Shadow
Tall reeds with golden halo-rings above them, dripping black water. They look holy but sad.

Gameplay: creates slowing puddles; if stepped in too long, shadow hands rise.

10. Furnace Lotus

Elements: Fire + Blood
A red-orange lotus floating in boiling cave water. Its center looks like a tiny furnace-heart.

Gameplay: erupts steam pillars and boiling blood bubbles.

11. Mirrorbark Shardtree

Elements: Light + Shadow
A small alien tree with reflective bark and black sap. Its branches bend like broken mirrors.

Gameplay: reflects projectiles at strange angles; weak from behind.

12. Gelmire Pitcher

Elements: Slime + Water + Shadow
A giant pitcher plant filled with dark gel. It smells sweet and glows faintly.

Gameplay: eats small enemies and spits them back as slime-coated projectiles.

13. Thorn-Script Ivy

Elements: Virethorn + Light
Ivy that grows in glowing writing across cave walls. The letters twitch like living insects.

Gameplay: glyph hazard; touching certain “letters” triggers thorn bursts.

14. Bloodroot Choir

Elements: Blood + Light + Virethorn
A cluster of red roots shaped like throats. They hum softly and pulse together.

Gameplay: buffs nearby plant enemies; the player should destroy it first.

15. Nullseed Pod

Elements: Void + Slime
A floating seed pod with a jelly membrane and empty black center.

Gameplay: drifts slowly; when destroyed, creates a temporary gravity well.

16. Crystal Lung Flower

Elements: Water + Light + Blood
A flower with two transparent lung-like sacs instead of petals. It breathes mist into the room.

Gameplay: releases crystal fog that hides enemies and damages slowly.

17. Shadowcap Choir Fungus

Elements: Shadow + Slime
A mushroom colony with tiny mouths under each cap. They sing without moving.

Gameplay: sends invisible sound waves along the ground.

18. Leviathan Door Kelp

Elements: Water + Void + Shadow
Long kelp strands with little door-shaped growths along them. The doors open to impossible dark water.

Gameplay: tentacle attacks come from one door and exit another.

19. Goldthorn Saintbloom

Elements: Light + Virethorn
A kneeling plant shaped like a saint with a flower halo. Beautiful, still, and unsettling.

Gameplay: appears passive, then opens into a thorn cage if approached.

20. Marrowvine Reliquary

Elements: Bone Bloom + Slime + Light
A vine system that carries bones inside clear slime tubes. The bones float like relics.

Gameplay: summons bone needles; destroying the slime tubes disables attacks.

21. Cinderfruit Tree

Elements: Fire + Virethorn
A thorny tree that grows burning fruit. The fruit drops and rolls like bombs.

Gameplay: environmental hazard; fruit can damage enemies too.

22. Dreammold Veil

Elements: Shadow + Void + Slime
A soft purple mold that grows over statues, walls, and dead enemies. It has closed eyes all over it.

Gameplay: if disturbed, eyes open and the screen darkens around the player.

23. Bloodglass Rose

Elements: Blood + Light
A rose made of red crystal petals. Its stem is a vein.

Gameplay: shoots sharp crystal petals in radial bursts; petals stick in walls.

24. Abyssal Sunflower

Elements: Light + Void
A sunflower with a bright rim and a perfectly black center. It always faces the player.

Gameplay: charges a beam while pulling the player toward its line of fire.

25. Mirethorn Creeping Altar

Elements: Water + Virethorn + Bone Bloom
A low wet plant that grows into altar shapes with bone candles.

Gameplay: creates enemy spawn points unless cut down quickly.

26. Slime Saint’s Cradle

Elements: Slime + Light + Blood
A soft glowing plant cradle holding tiny slime embryos. It rocks by itself.

Gameplay: spawns small slime saints; if purified, it could become a healing plant.

27. Blackfire Bramble

Elements: Fire + Shadow + Virethorn
A bramble bush burning with cold black flame. Its thorns smoke instead of glow.

Gameplay: damages on contact; periodically shoots black fire along vines.

28. Astral Vein Grass

Elements: Light + Water + Void
Thin glowing grass that grows along cracks in the cave floor. Each blade contains tiny flowing stars.

Gameplay: normally decorative, but in combat it can become a mana/stardust hazard or recharge point depending on the room.

29. Bonepetal Trapjaw

Elements: Bone Bloom + Shadow
A flower with bone petals arranged like a bear trap. The inside is pitch black.

Gameplay: snaps shut on the player; must be baited open before attacking.

30. The Hollow Garden Heart

Elements: Light + Blood + Void + Virethorn
A large alien plant-core that looks like a glowing heart suspended inside a cage of roots, thorns, and floating petals. It is not fully evil — it seems wounded and confused.

Gameplay: mini-boss plant. It changes phases: healing light, blood thorn attacks, void pull, then desperate root armor.

1. Moonmilk Bellflower

Elements: Water + Light
A pale blue bellflower that drips glowing “moonmilk” from its petals. The drops float upward instead of falling. Cats are strangely calm around it.

2. Void-Pearl Kelp

Elements: Void + Water
Long black kelp strands with pearl-like bubbles growing along them. Each pearl contains a tiny starless sky. When disturbed, the pearls blink like eyes.

3. Bloodglass Weeping Tree

Elements: Blood + Light
A small red crystal tree that constantly leaks bright crimson sap. The sap hardens into glass tears around its roots.

4. Saint’s Rib Fern

Elements: Bone Bloom + Light
A fern shaped like tiny rib bones, each rib tipped with a soft golden glow. It looks like a plant trying to become a chapel.

5. Ashveil Lotus

Elements: Fire + Shadow
A black lotus with smoky petals that never fully burn away. Its center glows orange like a coal hidden under funeral cloth.

6. Slimewomb Pod

Elements: Slime + Blood + Water
A translucent hanging pod with curled alien embryos inside. They are not animals yet, not plants anymore — just soft sleeping possibilities.

7. Star-Script Moss

Elements: Light + Void
Moss that grows in constellations and unreadable letters. When the player walks near it, the stars rearrange like it is trying to write a message.

8. Virethorn Mourning Willow

Elements: Virethorn + Shadow + Water
A drooping thorn tree with wet black leaves. Its branches hang like veils, and the thorns look like little hooks for catching prayers.

9. Crystal Lung Reed

Elements: Water + Light + Blood
Tall reeds with transparent sacs that inflate and deflate like lungs. Their breath makes the cave shimmer with red-blue mist.

10. Black Halo Mushroom

Elements: Shadow + Light
A mushroom with a glowing stem and a flat black cap surrounded by a thin golden ring. It looks sacred from far away, but wrong up close.

11. Leviathan’s Doorvine

Elements: Water + Void + Shadow
A vine that grows tiny door-shaped leaves. Sometimes one opens and shows deep ocean behind it, even in a dry cave.

12. Cinderfruit Rosary

Elements: Fire + Virethorn
A thorny vine with glowing bead-like fruits arranged like a rosary. Each fruit pulses with ember light.

13. Hollow-Eye Orchid

Elements: Void + Light
A white orchid whose center is a perfect black circular hole. Looking at it too long feels like looking into an eye that has forgotten how to see.

14. Marrowlily’s Dark Cousin

Elements: Slime + Bone Bloom + Shadow
A beautiful slime-flower with bone petals and black jelly roots. It is not evil, but it feels lonely and hungry.

15. Bloodroot Harpgrass

Elements: Blood + Light + Virethorn
Thin red grass that grows in harp-string shapes. When cave wind passes through it, it hums like distant singing.

16. Astral Vein Cactus

Elements: Light + Void + Water
A blue-black cactus with glowing veins inside its flesh. Its spines are tiny crystal stars. It stores astral moisture from cracks in reality.

17. Glassmire Lily Pads

Elements: Water + Light + Slime
Floating lily pads made of slick glassy slime. They shimmer with oil-rainbow colors and leave glowing ripples in still water.

18. Shadow Choir Fungus

Elements: Shadow + Slime + Light
A colony of mushrooms with tiny mouths under their caps. They do not sing loudly — they whisper harmonies you almost understand.

19. Behemoth’s Root-Knuckle

Elements: Bone Bloom + Blood + Virethorn
A thick root formation shaped like a giant buried hand. Bone plates grow over the bark like armor.

20. Null-Pollen Dandelion

Elements: Void + Shadow
A black dandelion whose seeds look like tiny eclipses. When the seeds drift away, they leave little dark circles in the air.

21. Firewater Coral Bloom

Elements: Fire + Water
A cave coral that burns underwater. Blue flames curl along its red branches, but the water around it stays cold.

22. Prismatic Thorn Lily

Elements: Light + Virethorn
A sharp white flower with rainbow crystal thorns. It looks delicate but every petal edge is knife-thin.

23. Slime Saint’s Veil

Elements: Slime + Light
A hanging curtain of translucent plant-slime that glows softly like stained glass. It sways like fabric and sometimes forms a kneeling silhouette.

24. Bloodmoon Pitcher

Elements: Blood + Shadow + Water
A deep red pitcher plant with a dark crescent mark inside. Its nectar reflects a moon that is not visible in the cave.

25. Oculoseed Bloom

Elements: Light + Shadow + Blood
A flower that grows eyeball-like seeds around its center. The seeds blink independently and follow movement.

26. Virethorn Reliquary Tree

Elements: Virethorn + Bone Bloom + Light
A small tree that grows bone charms, cracked halos, and thorn cages instead of fruit. It feels like a natural shrine made by a grieving cave.

27. Dreammold Cathedral

Elements: Slime + Shadow + Void
A huge patch of soft purple mold that grows into tiny cathedral spires. Little closed eyes dot the surface like sleeping windows.

28. Stardust Drinking Fern

Elements: Light + Water
A fern that unfolds only when stardust is nearby. Its leaves are shaped like little cat ears and glow when watered.

29. Crimson Spine Orchid

Elements: Blood + Bone Bloom
An orchid growing from a curved spine-like stem. Its petals pulse red, opening and closing like a slow heartbeat.

30. The Quiet Garden Mouth

Elements: Void + Light + Slime + Virethorn
A massive alien flower buried in the floor like a garden-shaped mouth. It does not attack. It simply opens sometimes, revealing soft light, black roots, and a sound like breathing under the world.

## Production rule

Plant entries should specify: area, visual silhouette, element blend, whether it is decorative/hazard/enemy, and whether it has Virethorn/Bone Bloom/Leviathan/astral significance.
