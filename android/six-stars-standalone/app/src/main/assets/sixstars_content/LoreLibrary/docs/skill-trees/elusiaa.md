# Elusiaa – Light Domain

“Mercy is how we remember we’re not gods.”

- Weapon: Radiant staff and light-infused claws/sword

## Root Node – Bond of Light (Passive) +8 Light Resistance +25 Mana Soft aura reveals hidden shrines, Stardust Nodes, and relics in darkness.

## Path 1: Dawn’s Embrace – Restoration & Renewal

### Minor Section 1

- Gentle Radiance: +5 Attack

- Flow of Dawn: +30 Mana

- Soothing Presence: +8 Defense

- Heart’s Resilience: +10 Max Health

### Star Skill 1 – LUMINOUS TOUCH (Level 3, 90 JP)

- Mana Version: 45% Mana

- Stardust Version: 40 Stardust Quick self-heal. Restores 20% of your Health. “Even the smallest light can mend a wounded heart.”

### Minor Section 2 (Stardust Focus)

- Stardust Bloom: +10% Stardust from basic attacks

- Dawn’s Rhythm: +15% Stardust from melee → sword switches

- Merciful Flow: +12% Stardust from healing skills

- Light’s Cadence: +20% Stardust from melee → skill switches

### Star Skill 2 – SERAPH’S CASCADE (Level 5, 280 JP)

- Mana Version: 55 Mana

- Stardust Version: 50 Stardust Wispy Light projectile barrage. Fires three sets of three glowing doves that fly forward and arc upward, dealing consistent Light damage.

### Minor Section 3 (Astral Expression Synergy)

- Astral Radiance: +15% Stardust version power in Astral Expression Mode

- Stardust Harmony: +25% Stardust gained in Astral Expression Mode

- Echo of Mercy: Luminous Touch Stardust cost reduced by 30% in Astral Expression Mode

- Dawn’s Insight: +10% critical chance in Astral Expression Mode

### Star Skill 3 – Eternal Benediction (Level 7, 520 JP) – New

- Mana Version: 65 Mana

- Stardust Version: 60 Stardust Creates a large lingering golden field that grants HoT and damage reduction.

### Minor Section 4

- Divine Flow: +12% healing and HoT strength

- Stardust Purity: +15% Stardust from purified enemies

- Light Eternal: +25 max Stardust

- Astral Mercy: Dawn Eternal gains extra effects when cast with Stardust in Astral Expression Mode

## Path Capstone – Dawn Eternal (Level 9, 920 JP)

- Mana Version: 75% Mana

- Stardust Version: 80 Stardust Ultimate self-restoration. Fully restores Health, removes all debuffs, and grants “Eternal Dawn” (+25% healing received, +12% all damage for 12s).
