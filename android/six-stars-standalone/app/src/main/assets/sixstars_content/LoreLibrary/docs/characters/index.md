# Characters Index

Canonical Star-safe people, companions, guardians, settlements, and Six Stars reference. Hidden endgame identities and private creator notes are intentionally excluded.

## The Last Cat

## Identity

The Last Cat is the player character, a small white cat and the first new astral conduit seen in a century. He appeared twenty years ago at an intergalactic orphanage and grew up with mysterious letters from people who seemed to know and love him.

## Role

He is the bridge between the old cat sage warriors and the future Rescue the Stars universe. He starts as a baby sage warrior and can become the Astral Dragoth, a galaxy-restoring conduit whose power may surpass angelic mana systems.

## Powers

- Natural stardust generation.
- Star ability attunement.
- No-mana miracle learning.
- Soul sword use.
- Astral Expression Mode.
- Potential Astral Dragoth transformation.

## Weakness

Water causes stardust leakage. This is both a lore statement and a gameplay rule.

## Personality

Humble, curious, resilient, occasionally sassy, cute enough to make the world underestimate him, and powerful enough to change cosmic history.

## Marrowlily

Marrowlily is the slime pet born from the Bone Bloom/Slime Fragment progression. She is cute, strange, morally important, and a reminder that slimes are not merely food, enemies, or resources.

## Gameplay roles

- Companion pet.
- Helps read inscriptions or sense astral remnants.
- Can learn spit attacks or slime utility powers.
- May be temporarily possessed/boosted by lesser spirits in specific combat systems.

## Design purpose

Marrowlily balances horror with tenderness. She keeps the player emotionally connected to the suffering of slimes and the possibility that corrupted creatures can still become friends.

## Bradmageddon

## Status

Bradmageddon is a private/late/meta-lore figure and should be handled carefully. He can be used as a decoy villain, narrator, secret organizer, or the frightening voice of a wider mission.

## Function in the Story

Bradmageddon may appear after the large slime/Bone Bloom arc and initially seem responsible for demonic invasions or slime possession.

Possible line:

> “Yes, I sent the demons to possess the slimes here long ago, for this has been planned and is needed.”

He may not tell the Last Cat he is not the real enemy. The point is to give the player a manageable enemy-shaped idea before facing the more terrifying possibility of raising, judging, or fighting a young angel.

## Rescue the Stars Role

In the long-term MMO vision, Bradmageddon and the team can function like an organization that protects and honors angelic interests by letting art, offerings, and community contributions become content if they pass the world’s tests.

## Tone

He should sound villainous, fiery, strange, and provocative, even when serving a hidden good.

Possible line:

> “Shall we wrestle, humans and angels, or do the forces of good require evil hands to rip them to pieces?”

## Behemoth / Bahamut / Bone Bloom

## Identity

Behemoth/Bahamut is interpreted in the game as an internal-strength guardian. Bone Bloom is the major in-game manifestation of this theme: a life-force guardian whose body is flesh, bone, marrow, and astral core.

## Role

Bone Bloom guards life force. It tests whether the Last Cat can build something good from death, carry bones without becoming cruel, and recognize that grotesque guardians may protect hidden life.

## Thematic meaning

- Loins and belly: internal strength and ability to bear/digest suffering.
- Bones like bronze: protection of marrow/life force.
- Marsh/lotus imagery: beauty rising from decay.
- Bridge-building: making a path through rot instead of simply destroying rot.

## Gameplay

- Major Bone Caves boss.
- Drops or releases the Slime Fragment / core that becomes Marrowlily.
- Can unlock letters/cutscenes when addressed with guardian writings.

## Leviathan

## Identity

## Role in game

At first Leviathan tests the Last Cat with accusation:

> "Your kind sealed the flow... and now it is time to unseal you."

Later this is revealed as a veiled prophecy. He is not merely blaming cats. He is telling the Astral Stream that the time has come to unseal the Astral Dragoth within the Last Cat.

## Guardian meaning

Leviathan represents external authority, holy speech, scales of unity, unbreakable heart, and the ability to turn chaotic seas into healing ointment.

## Gameplay function

- Mentor for no-mana astral power.
- Gatekeeper of the Astral Stream.
- Source of soul sword and ancient cat revelations.
- Possible companion/favor system.
- Revealer of hidden cat sanctuary.

## Hidden cats

Leviathan transported the cats to a place between planets and galaxies, where a moving sun follows every living soul. This sanctuary appears in times of great need.

## Cave Alien Races

These are alien races to meet in the cave network. They should feel like cultures, not just enemy silhouettes.

## Starweavers

Astral-core people with tiny compact heart/mind/spirit/soul cores protected in high-tech chest devices. They may have replaced conventional brains with protected astral cores.

Visual direction:

- Compact glowing chest core.
- Colorful protective clothing.
- Male and female variants.
- Elegant, technical, star-thread culture.

Behavior:

- Study memory, destiny, and astral patterns.
- May help with the Astral Book or letter interpretation.
- Could live in upper/astral cave settlements.

## Marrowbound

Bone/flesh/blood-adjacent people connected to the Marrow Ossuary and Bone Bloom ecology.

Visual direction:

- Bone armor, marrow glow, grounded bodies.
- Strong feet and practical cave clothing.
- May look intimidating but not necessarily evil.

Behavior:

- Respect bones, bridges, burial, and old suffering.
- Could have complicated relationships with Maera and Bone Bloom.

## Veilshades

Shadow/void people tied to hidden rooms, unseen guardians, and Nyxari’s secrets.

Visual direction:

- Dark bodies with veil-like cloth and glowing eyes/cores.
- Male and female variants.
- Elegant, quiet, half-seen silhouettes.

Behavior:

- Trade in secrets.
- Know hidden guardian locations.
- May speak in riddles and half-truths.

## Hybrid Race Concept

A later race can combine elements of the ten alien creature concepts: owl, slime, Virethorn, astral core, shadow veil, high-tech chest device, and colorful protective outfits.

## Six Stars — Astral Dragoth Forms

Astral Dragoth forms are advanced transformations unlocked after mastering astral abilities. They show what happens when Star mana, astral independence, and guardian-beast resonance fuse into a new body language.

## Global Visual Rules

- Keep a balance of human/star beauty and dragon/dragoth features.
- Do not make them full monsters; they remain recognizable as the Stars.
- Dragon parts should read as magical anatomy, armor, horns, wings, tails, claws, or radiant/scaled limbs.
- Outfits can remain stylish and sensual, but should look like high-tech/mystic battle gear.
- Forms should be usable for high-tier cutscenes, special attacks, final stages, or late-game romance visuals.

## Kaelia — Void Dragoth

- Element: Void.
- Dragon coloration: lighter purple with black-violet cosmic shadows.
- Human features: light brown hair or dreads, green eyes, aloof expression.
- Details: DNA-like abdomen tattoo, slightly armored high-tech bra/top, human/dragoth skin ratio similar to the source concept.
- Mood: beautiful, dangerous, mysterious, secretly needy.

## Elusiaa — Light Dragoth

- Element: Light.
- Hair: blonde.
- Eyes: blue.
- Dragon features: white-gold scales, soft blue/teal holy glow, gentle halo motifs, luminous wings.
- Mood: mercy becoming majestic.

## Khavia — Water Dragoth

- Element: Water.
- Dragon features: blue Leviathan-like scales, flowing fins, luminous water ribbons, aquatic claws.
- Mood: friendship, currents, and bridge-making turned into a living sea-dragon form.

## Nyxari — Shadow Dragoth

- Element: Shadow.
- Hair: platinum or icy flame.
- Dragon features: midnight blue, deep red, platinum shadow-fire edges.
- Mood: secretive, elegant, fragmented, dangerous.

## Maera — Blood Dragoth

- Element: Blood.
- Dragon features: dark skull and flesh accents, crimson veins, bone ornaments, blood-crystal glow.
- Mood: possession purified into protection or twisted into obsession, depending on route.

## Auerlis — Fire Dragoth

- Element: Fire.
- Dragon features: flame hair, ember scales, phoenix/fire-dragon accents, hot orange-red aura.
- Mood: joy, laughter, and fire that warms — or consumes.

## Angel story

### Dark angel story — The Kind Face that Burned

Auerlis tells the Last Cat a secondhand story about an old spirit who barely escaped a dark angel wearing a kind face. The angel tore the spirit's memories apart, made him relive shame and failure, called that “love,” and left him believing no one was listening when he screamed for mercy.

**Gameplay use:** This story explains why Auerlis insists that some fires do not warm; they consume. It should make the Astral Caverns feel dangerous while preserving her role as the Star of Joy.

## Angel story

### Good angel story — The Angel in the Dying Valley

Elusiaa tells the Last Cat about a good angel who came to a drought-stricken valley as a quiet traveler with only a waterskin. He shared water, carried the weak, sang to children, and left after the rain came without seeking praise. His line, “The Stream flows for all who are thirsty,” defines Elusiaa's mercy ideal: angels were made to serve, not rule.

**Gameplay use:** This story should be a callback when Elusiaa is tempted to punish goblins or destroy a settlement. A wise player can remind her that the good angel sought water, truth, and service before judgment.

## Angel story

### Dark angel story — The Tar-Water Reflection

Kaelia tells the Last Cat that an older, hungrier dark angel came to her in the deep dark and told her the Void was all there was. He made her watch her own reflection drown again and again; whenever she reached for the surface, the water turned to tar. He laughed that even her own light rejected her.

**Gameplay use:** This story explains why Kaelia's mystery is a wound rather than a philosophy. Wise players should answer her despair without agreeing that the Void is all there is.

### Good angel story — The Angel Who Left a Gift Behind

Nyxari tells a complicated story about a good angel who lived inside a dead man's body for six years, healed the sick, protected the weak, and brought light to dying worlds. On the seventh year, the original man returned alive, young, strong, beloved, and able to continue the heroic work as himself. The angel's final message was, “Tell them even the worst of us can sometimes leave gifts behind.”

**Gameplay use:** This story belongs to Nyxari because it is about shadow, fragmentation, possession, identity, and a terrifying thing choosing to give instead of take.
