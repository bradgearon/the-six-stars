# Endings and MMO Carryover

## The Rewrite Finale

As the Last Cat speaks with Leviathan, Benaveil feels threatened by the Astral Stream's independent judgment. He rewrites the game into a Mega Man-style boss selection screen.

The player fights the Six Stars one by one. These are not rematches; they are forced first confrontations with companions. The Stars are angry, hurt, unruly, and accusing. Their good qualities are twisted against the player.

After the six stages, the player faces Benaveil.

## Hell Arcade Foreshadowing

The optional Hell arcade machine **The Six Stars** is the miniature warning version of the finale. It uses a black-and-white boss-select structure, **616** title/HP imagery, one-move Star fights, score pressure, and a hidden dark-winged Dragoth silhouette after all six Stars are cleared.

When Benaveil later rewrites the game, the player should recognize the shape of the event: what was once a strange, fun, low-resolution cabinet in Hell becomes a real Mega Man-style confrontation with the Stars and then Benaveil. The arcade was practice. The rewrite is the real battle.

Carryover hooks from the arcade can include Hell-only cosmetics, a black-and-white shader, score titles, rare Benaveil-fragment redemption flags, and Star dialogue acknowledging what the player saw.

## Final Fight Variants

### Duel / Unity Path

Benaveil allows equipped Star abilities and mana. This version is personal and can end with salvation, naming, and team unity.

### Tragic / Fight-to-Death Path

Benaveil denies the mana system or the player loses access to unlearned powers. Only fully learned astral versions remain. The player fights as an independent astral conduit, possibly in Astral Dragoth form.

### Benjudiel Tragic Name

In a tragic path, Benaveil can become **Benjudiel**, a name implying the veil lifted into a Judas-like tragic guardian identity. This is tied to judgment, self-condemnation, and “Let Us Call Hell.”

## Ending Archetypes

| Ending | Meaning | Carryover |
|---|---|---|
| Wise | Everyone saved. Highest redemption path. | Strongest unity server state. |
| Shrewd | Cats saved, player becomes godlike/independent. | Sage-cat prestige, more dangerous autonomy. |
| Kind/Fun | Benaveil and Stars become friends/team. | Best co-op adventure setup. |
| Tragic | Benaveil lost/killed/condemned, but hope may remain. | Scarred server state, dove symbol, rescue hook. |

## Last-Minute Chance

There should always be a final chance to save things unless the player deliberately chooses destruction. Losing Benaveil should feel like a true tragedy, not an accidental fail state.

## Life Recap

After the finale, show Benaveil's life and growth/fall based on choices. Explain what happened without moralizing too bluntly.

## Rescue the Stars Carryover

Carry to the persistent multiplayer world:

- Named Benaveil.
- Star ally/romance state.
- Star outfits and weapons/foci.
- Astral abilities mastered.
- Leviathan favor.
- Marrowlily progression.
- Ending type and world flavor.
- Soulbound token / sage status for unity ending.

## Persistent Multiplayer Vision

Rescue the Stars supports up to sixteen players and many planets. Players train cats, fight angels, rescue people, explore massive beasts, earn treasure, and continue growing as sage warrior cats.

Dream target:

- 120 bosses and incredible dungeons long-term.
- Community-submitted art/content accepted as story contribution when valuable.
- Servers shaped by endings and player legacies.
