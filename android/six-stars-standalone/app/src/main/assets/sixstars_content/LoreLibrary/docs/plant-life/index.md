# Plant Life Index

The plant-life library includes alien flora, dangerous flowers, Virethorn growths, astral garden pieces, and Bone Bloom-adjacent life.

## Core plant families

- Virethorn corruption plants.
- False holy saint plants.
- Bone Bloom sprouts and marrow flora.
- Astral cave flowers and eye-flowers.
- Void orchids and null-pollen plants.
- Bloodroot, bloodglass, and crimson spine plants.
- Slime cradles, slimeglass ferns, and gelmire pitcher plants.
