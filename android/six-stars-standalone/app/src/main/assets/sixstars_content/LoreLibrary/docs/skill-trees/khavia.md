# Khavia – Water Domain

“Somebody helped me once.”

- Weapon: Astral Tidechain (living water chain)

## Core Mechanics

### Star Skills can be cast with Mana or Stardust (same effect).

Stardust is generated by attacking enemies and especially by performing melee → sword or melee → skill combos. Switching between them grants significantly more Stardust.

Astral Expression Mode reduces Stardust costs and causes attacks to generate Mana while active.

## Root Node – Bond of the Tide (Passive) +8 Water Resistance +30 Mana Leaves lingering water bonds that slightly slow enemies and restore minor Mana over time.
