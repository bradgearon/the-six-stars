# Cave Alien Races

These are alien races to meet in the cave network. They should feel like cultures, not just enemy silhouettes.

## Starweavers

Astral-core people with tiny compact heart/mind/spirit/soul cores protected in high-tech chest devices. They may have replaced conventional brains with protected astral cores.

Visual direction:

- Compact glowing chest core.
- Colorful protective clothing.
- Male and female variants.
- Elegant, technical, star-thread culture.

Behavior:

- Study memory, destiny, and astral patterns.
- May help with the Astral Book or letter interpretation.
- Could live in upper/astral cave settlements.

## Marrowbound

Bone/flesh/blood-adjacent people connected to the Marrow Ossuary and Bone Bloom ecology.

Visual direction:

- Bone armor, marrow glow, grounded bodies.
- Strong feet and practical cave clothing.
- May look intimidating but not necessarily evil.

Behavior:

- Respect bones, bridges, burial, and old suffering.
- Could have complicated relationships with Maera and Bone Bloom.

## Veilshades

Shadow/void people tied to hidden rooms, unseen guardians, and Nyxari’s secrets.

Visual direction:

- Dark bodies with veil-like cloth and glowing eyes/cores.
- Male and female variants.
- Elegant, quiet, half-seen silhouettes.

Behavior:

- Trade in secrets.
- Know hidden guardian locations.
- May speak in riddles and half-truths.

## Hybrid Race Concept

A later race can combine elements of the ten alien creature concepts: owl, slime, Virethorn, astral core, shadow veil, high-tech chest device, and colorful protective outfits.
