Meeting Kaelia (Before Virethorn Water Puzzle)

Kaelia steps out from behind a cluster of blackened coral, her robes drifting like liquid shadow in the water. She studies you with intense, unblinking curiosity.

Kaelia: "...So you're the one the Stream has been whispering about. The Last Cat. How strange... a warm little light walking through these dying caves."

She circles you slowly, her voice low and intimate.

Kaelia: "I met one of them once. one of the older, hungrier ones. He came to me in the deep dark when I was still young. He told me the Void was all there was. That I was already damned, so I might as well rule the darkness instead of begging for light that would never come. That’s why I serve the darkness because I vowed to do a better job than him."

Her eyes narrow, a flicker of old pain crossing her face.

Kaelia: "He made me watch my own reflection drown over and over. Every time I tried to reach for the surface, the water turned to tar and pulled me back down. For weeks I felt like I was suffocating even when I was breathing. He laughed and said, 'See? Even your own light rejects you.'"

She stops circling and looks directly at you, her tone darker.

Kaelia: "I still hear him sometimes when the water gets too still. Be careful, little cat. The dark angels don't just attack the body. They attack the hope that anything good was ever meant for you."

Last Cat: "...I'm sorry that happened to you."

Kaelia (soft, almost vulnerable for a moment): "Sorry doesn't empty the tar from the water. But... you being here makes the current feel a little less heavy. Maybe that's something."

Meeting Maera (Flesh and Bones Area, Before Bone Bloom)

The area is grotesque — walls made of fused bone and pulsing flesh, skulls embedded in the stone. You are carrying heavy bones to build a bridge when Maera appears from a ribcage-like alcove, her red and black robes stained with old blood.

Maera (voice rough, emotional, with barely contained rage):

"You... you're the one they've been talking about. The cat from the stars."

She watches you struggle with the bones for a moment before speaking again.

Maera: "I met one of his forms in the blood caves years ago. He wore the flesh in my mind of a big black beast. He told me the blood I carried was proof I was already cursed. That every drop I spilled was just payment for existing. Then he made me feel like my own veins were full of worms — crawling, biting, eating me from the inside while I was awake."

Her fists clench, blood-like energy flickering around her hands.

Maera: "He made me watch myself bleed out over and over in visions. Every time I tried to stop the bleeding, the wounds opened somewhere else. He whispered that this was mercy — showing me what I really was before the final judgment came. I begged him to stop. He laughed and said even my begging was ugly."

She looks at you, her expression fierce but cracked with old pain.

Maera: "I still feel them sometimes... the worms under my skin when the caves get too quiet. That's what the dark angels do. They don't just hurt you. They make you hate the fact that you're alive."

Last Cat: "...That sounds like hell."

Maera (bitter laugh): "It was. But you're here now, carrying bones like some fool who thinks he can build something good out of all this rot. Maybe you're crazier than I am. Or maybe... that's exactly what we need. One day I know in my bones I will make them bleed out. I will make every last one of them bleed out."

Meeting Auerlis (Entrance to the Astral Caverns)

The moment you step into the glowing Astral Caverns, Auerlis appears in a burst of fire and shadow, floating above a crystal outcrop. Her fiery hair and orange-red robes flicker like living flame.

Auerlis (voice intense, passionate, with underlying fear):

"You... the Last Cat. I heard the Stream singing about you. I didn't believe it until I saw you with my own eyes."

She descends, flames licking at her feet, but her expression is troubled.

Auerlis: "I heard a story from an old spirit who barely escaped one of the dark angels. He said the angel came to him in the middle of the night wearing a kind face. Then he tore the spirit's memories apart — made him relive every failure, every shame, every time he had tried and fallen. The angel whispered that this was love — showing him the truth so he would stop hoping for something better. The spirit said he felt like he was burning alive from the inside for weeks."

Her flames flare brighter for a moment, then dim.

Auerlis: "He said the angel's final words were, 'Even if you scream for mercy, no one is listening. Not even the One who made you.' The spirit still wakes up screaming sometimes. He says the worst part wasn't the pain — it was believing he deserved it."

She looks at you directly, her fiery eyes intense.

Auerlis: "That's what waits in these deeper caves. Dark angels who don't just want to kill you. They want you to thank them for it. Be careful, little cat. Some fires don't warm. They only consume."

Last Cat: "...I'll be careful. Thank you for telling me."

Auerlis (softer, almost hopeful): "Maybe you're the one who can walk through the fire without burning. I hope so. Because if even one cat can make it... maybe the rest of us still have a chance."

## 1. Elusiaa – Early Game (First Area, After helping goblin and harpy settlement)

Elusiaa kneels gracefully before you, her light gentle and warm in the dim Virethorn caves.

Elusiaa: "Little one… I once saw a good angel in a dying valley. The people had lost everything to a long drought. Instead of appearing in glory, he came as a quiet traveler with nothing but a small waterskin. He walked among them for weeks, sharing what little water he had, helping carry the weak, and singing soft songs to the children at night."

She smiles with quiet wonder.

Elusiaa: "When the rains finally came, the people said the water tasted sweeter than any they had known before. The angel did not stay for praise. He simply said, ‘The Stream flows for all who are thirsty,’ and continued on his way.

It was the first time I understood that some angels still remember they were made to serve, not to rule."

Last Cat: "That sounds like real kindness."

Elusiaa (warmly): "It was. And now I see another small light walking these caves. Perhaps the Stream is sending more like him… or perhaps you are one."

Later Bradmaggedon:

Elusiaa told you about the angel in the dying valley. They say the people in that valley used to burn harpies alive on stakes when they found them on that planet. The harpies there are known for their wisdom, and they have helped many other planets with big problems.

## 2. Khavia – Second Area (After Building the Final Bone Bridge & Confronting Bone Bloom)

Khavia stands near the newly restored bone bridge, her red and black robes still stained from the work. She looks at you with a mix of surprise and respect.

Khavia: "You actually did it. You faced Bone Bloom and built something good from all this death."

She pauses, then speaks more softly.

Khavia: "I once witnessed a good angel in the blood-red canyons. A terrible battle had left many spirits broken and dying. The angel did not come to fight. He came afterward. He sat in the dirt with the wounded, binding their wounds with his own hands, singing over them until their pain eased.

One dying warrior asked him why he would help enemies. The angel replied, ‘There are no enemies here — only the hurt and the lost. And I was sent to the lost.’"

Her voice grows quieter, almost hopeful.

Khavia: "He stayed until every wounded spirit could stand again. Then he left without a word. The canyon felt… lighter afterward.

I never forgot that. Some angels still choose healing over power."

Last Cat: "That’s beautiful."

Khavia (small smile): "It is. And now a small cat is doing the same — carrying bones, building bridges, refusing to leave the broken places. Maybe the old kindness isn’t dead after all."

Later Bradmaggedon: I know you met Khavia and she told you about the angel in the blood red canyons. I heard the lizard people of that planet were atrocious. They kept their young in cages in their homes and forced them to do all the work of the home. Many lived dark lives outside of the law and unchecked because that planet never developed a justice system. I’m not sure if this is true but as always one never knows what happens behind the scenes.

## 3. Nyxari – Middle of the Astral Caverns

Nyxari emerges from a swirling shadow near a glowing crystal formation. Her midnight-blue and deep red robes shift like living darkness, but her expression is softer than usual.

Nyxari: "The Last Cat. The one the shadows have been whispering about."

She studies you for a moment, then speaks with rare openness.

Nyxari: "I heard a story from an old alien spirit who met a good angel. For six years the angel lived inside a good man’s body. He did beautiful, magical things — healed the sick, protected the weak, brought light to dying worlds. Everyone loved him.

On the seventh year, the real man — who had been dead for one full day before the possession — suddenly returned. He woke up young again, strong, beautiful, and deeply loved by everyone who had known the angel’s version of him. He continued the heroic work, but now it was truly him."

Her voice grows quieter, almost wondering.

Nyxari: "The spirit who told me said the angel’s last words were, ‘Tell them even the worst of us can sometimes leave gifts behind.’

It was the first time I believed that some angels can still choose to give instead of take."

Last Cat: "That’s… complicated but hopeful."

Nyxari (soft smile): "It is. And now a small cat walks these caves carrying hope in his very presence. Maybe the light isn’t as far away as we thought."
