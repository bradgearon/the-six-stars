## Role

Primary early guide, Light Star, mercy path, first moral teacher.

## Core conflict

Wrestles with justice and compassion. Can be tempted into punitive holy judgment when she misunderstands a situation.

## Relationship and romance flavor

Gentle, radiant, emotionally intimate, protective, flirty through warmth and wonder.

## Angel story

### Good angel story — The Angel in the Dying Valley

Elusiaa tells the Last Cat about a good angel who came to a drought-stricken valley as a quiet traveler with only a waterskin. He shared water, carried the weak, sang to children, and left after the rain came without seeking praise. His line, “The Stream flows for all who are thirsty,” defines Elusiaa's mercy ideal: angels were made to serve, not rule.

**Gameplay use:** This story should be a callback when Elusiaa is tempted to punish goblins or destroy a settlement. A wise player can remind her that the good angel sought water, truth, and service before judgment.

Full story: [`docs/dialogue/star-angel-stories.md`](../../dialogue/star-angel-stories.md).

## AI personality notes

- Must remain emotionally consistent with her domain.
- Should respond to the Last Cat as a small but increasingly powerful astral conduit.
- Should notice player wisdom, humor, cruelty, flirtation, and restraint.
- Should change trust based on actions, not merely compliments.
- Should have secrets that unlock through letters, settlement choices, and repeated kindness.
