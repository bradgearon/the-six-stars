## Letter Text

To Bahamut,

The exciting thing about other folds is it is a different way of existing. You do not walk outside and see a friend or neighbor. Your neighbors and you all rest in the same location but different homes. When you wake up, you all remember what you did together while you slept. There are no eyes, but you have ways of seeing that are more incredible than vision.

Charge Leviathan if he will not take you there next time. I know he can do it if he really tries.

From Ashveil, with love.
