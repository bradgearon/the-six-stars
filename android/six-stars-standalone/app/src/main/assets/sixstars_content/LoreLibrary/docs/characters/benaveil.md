# Benaveil

## Identity

Benaveil is the young angel behind the Stars' mana system. His name is purpose-like, in the style of a “name of God” angelic title, more than a personal name at first.

He is genuinely young. The story is about a baby angel and a baby sage-warrior cat growing together, failing together, and either becoming a family symphony or ending in tragedy.

## Public Spoiler Rule

In public-facing marketing and early-game experience, do not reveal Benaveil too clearly. Players may believe they are facing Satan, a Satan-like force, a dark angel, or “something worse.”

The truth of Benaveil should unfold gradually.

## Forms

- Named radiant forms: guide the Stars, provide mana, appear loving and purposeful.
- Unnamed/carnal dark form: invasive, hungry, horror-coded, empowered by greater evils.
- Tragic named form: **Benjudiel**, used in a tragic/Judas-like path.

## Relationship to the Stars

The Stars are spiritual children/forms/extensions of his power and history, not literal children. Their mana comes through his system. They can learn freedom through the Last Cat's astral mastery.

## Central Fear

Benaveil fears independent astral power because it bypasses his mana control. Leviathan terrifies him because Leviathan knows the Astral Stream and may know Benaveil's judgment.

## Naming Mechanic

At some point the player may help give Benaveil an actual name. That name is saved and carried forward into Rescue the Stars.

## Endgame

Benaveil rewrites the game when the Last Cat speaks with Leviathan. He forces the player to fight the Six Stars, then faces the player himself.

Possible outcomes:

- Saved and named.
- Becomes ally/team member.
- Condemned or killed.
- Lost in hell but still reachable through prayer/gameplay hope.
- Becomes Benjudiel in the tragic path.

## Design Tone

Benaveil must feel terrifying, deep, powerful, wounded, and capable of real evil. But he must also feel young enough that the player can believe he might grow.
