# Six Star Magical Weapons and Equipment

## Equipment Slots

Both the Last Cat and Stars can have visual/equipment slots. Star gear changes appearance and gives stats or ability modifiers, but Stars do not need conventional HP/defense balancing like the player.

Suggested slots:

- Head / Hat / Crown
- Chest
- Arms
- Legs
- Feet
- Weapon / Magical Focus

## Design Goal

The Stars should feel like powerful sorceresses, not melee warriors. Their “weapons” are magical foci: staff, orb, codex, lantern, crystal, mirror, etc.

## Upgrade Direction

Each focus can evolve with:

- Star relationship.
- Astral version learned.
- Outfit set completion.
- Girlfriend status.
- Endgame rewrite route.
- Dragoth mastery.

## Maera Focus Detail — Sanguine Heart

Sanguine Heart is a living crimson crystal that floats beside Maera and pulses like a heartbeat. Veins of blood magic swirl inside. It should look beautiful and dangerous, like love and ownership turned into a gemstone.
