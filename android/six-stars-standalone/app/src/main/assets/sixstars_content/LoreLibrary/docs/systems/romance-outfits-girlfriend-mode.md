# Romance, Outfits, and Girlfriend Mode

## Design position

The Stars are designed to be beautiful, sexy, charismatic, and emotionally compelling. The game should not be embarrassed by this. A major target appeal is collecting outfits for powerful attractive women, feeling flirted with by them, and building relationships that matter in combat and story.

## Toggle

Romance/Girlfriend Mode should be controlled in options.

- **Off:** strong friendship, emotional loyalty, no overt romantic pursuit.
- **On:** flirtation, romantic banter, jealousy/comedy, deeper intimacy, girlfriend talent trees, stronger visual summon emphasis.
- **Intensity slider:** recommended for future accessibility and audience control.

## Outfit system

Outfits are collected piece by piece:

- Hat/headpiece
- Chest/robe/top
- Arms/gloves
- Legs/skirt/pants
- Feet/boots
- Weapon/focus

Outfits provide visual variation and stats. Star outfits should not add normal HP/defense as if they are always physically present; they should enhance spell visuals, synergy, romance scenes, summon animations, and passives.

## Girlfriend outcome

Committing to one Star can unlock:

- unique dialogue and greetings;
- enhanced combat summon animations;
- unique relationship quests;
- special outfit variants;
- synergy bonuses;
- dedicated job-point talent tree;
- ending variants;
- MMO carry-over identity.

## Consent and tone rules

- Flirtation should feel mutual.
- Stars can reject or redirect the player.
- Pushy or manipulative player behavior should reduce trust.
- Sexy does not require explicit sex scenes.
- The game can be horny, funny, and dark without losing emotional dignity.
