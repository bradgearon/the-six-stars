## Letter Text

To the Last Cat,

Everyone at school is watching you.

Nobody knows what to say anymore because none of us have ever heard stories like yours before. Some of us think you are a hero. Some of us think you are a myth.

We started clubs to talk about the magic we are all supposed to have soon.

Every week somebody claims they already have it.

I cannot wait until you get here.

~ A student
