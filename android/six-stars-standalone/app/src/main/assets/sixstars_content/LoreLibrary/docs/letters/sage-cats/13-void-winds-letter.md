## Letter Text

Dear Behemoth,

I saw the void swirling around some winds earlier. They had torn an entire village apart on a planet about fourteen purrs away. I am not sure what happened after that, but those winds used to have names.

Maybe they will return, maybe not.

The Astral Stream knows already.

Ask him.

With love,

Saberthorn

## Developer Notes

Can support a later quest where the player chooses whether a destructive force can be renamed/restored.
