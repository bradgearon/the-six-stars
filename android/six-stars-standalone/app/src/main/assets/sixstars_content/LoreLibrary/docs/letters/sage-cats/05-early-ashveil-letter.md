## Letter Text

To Bahamut,

A hungry void eats action, but a thirsty void drinks strength.

Technically I am a cat, but I have the strangest relationship with water. When I run beneath it, I tickle. When other cats see droplets on my fur, their eyes sparkle as though they recognize something I do not.

I wonder if all of us know this.

This is the first of many letters I am writing and leaving on this planet.

I think cats might have started here.

Further research is required.

From Ashveil, for science.
