## Letter Text

Dear friend,

Do not let them sell you stardust.

Mana is a counterfeit light, and the dark ones have started calling their friends the Stars to mock us.

Listen to Leviathan.

He is closer to the Stream than most of us will ever be.

Please be careful.

~ A concerned mother

## Developer Notes

This is a childhood letter the Last Cat brought with him, not a cave pickup.
