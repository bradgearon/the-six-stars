## Letter Text

To Bahamut,

Fold over the biscuits and listen. This fold is slower than the rest.

For a long time machines have ruled over the worst of us in torment. Without your roaring and the legs of your youth, you have become more useful. The lesser ones here tremble when you rise and they stay inside when it is most hot in the ancient belly.

The beast is hungry for what is inside of him. Shall we rouse hell itself in thirst again? Can the less be made even lesser without our grumbles.

From Ashveil, for science
