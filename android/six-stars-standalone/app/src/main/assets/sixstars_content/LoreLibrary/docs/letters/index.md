# Letters Index

Letters are a central progression system. They are not just lore pickups; they teach wisdom, affect Star relationships, unlock ally paths, support romance, point toward swords, and reveal the deeper guardian/beast truths.

This update promotes the Grok letter material from the raw bank into **21 individual markdown letter entries**:

- **18 primary/canon-or-fragment letters** from the user’s consolidated list and identifiable fragments.
- **3 additional recovered variants/candidates** from the Grok input archive: Flameveil’s two Leviathan letters and the high-tech Bahamut “Fold Over the Biscuits” draft.

## Canon Folders

- [`last-cat/`](last-cat/index.md) — childhood letters, carried clues, and direct letters to the Last Cat.
- [`cat-sanctuary/`](cat-sanctuary/index.md) — messages from hidden cats or sanctuary voices.
- [`sage-cats/`](sage-cats/index.md) — Ashveil, Saberthorn/Sablethorn, Archstride, Flameveil-adjacent sage material.
- [`guardians/`](guardians/index.md) — index and mapping for letters addressed to Leviathan, Bahamut/Behemoth, Bone Bloom, or the guardian class.
- [`prophetic-endgame/`](prophetic-endgame/index.md) — high-stakes prophecy, hell, final judgment, and Astral Book letters.
- [`fragments-and-variants/`](fragments-and-variants/index.md) — short fragments and draft variants preserved so they are not lost.
- [`settlements/`](settlements/index.md) — future settlement-authored letters.
- [`grok-letter-bank.md`](grok-letter-bank.md) — raw imported source material.

## Return Targets

Letters can be returned to:

- Bone Bloom / Bahamut before and after his arc.
- Leviathan once accessible.
- The Astral Book in save rooms.
- Specific Stars when the letter concerns their domain.

## Effects

- Star ally recruitment.
- Star romance trust.
- Benaveil alignment pressure.
- Secret sword hints.
- Guardian cutscenes.
- Wisdom path scoring.
- Astral Stream understanding.

## Important Authors

- Ashveil — “For Science,” “With Wonder,” and “With Flames/War” modes.
- Saberthorn / Sablethorn — fierce warning, fallen-thing listening, void-wind reports.
- Archstride — gentle, whimsical, beauty/crystal/wind observations.
- Flameveil — fire, war, memory, and accountability toward Leviathan.
- Dying mothers, concerned parents, students, and hidden cats.
