## Letter Text

To Leviathan,

I see you.

You were made to bear much.

Scale the deepest sea and make it honey. This would make your friends happy.

The fires have always raged, and yet we are still alone within them. Every sea you cross becomes too small, so you flee to find another.

Stop that, dear child.

Not every horizon is an invitation.

Some are a place to rest.

From Ashveil, with wonder.

## Developer Notes

This is one of the most important guide letters: it rebukes Leviathan without humiliating him.
