The Stream always said if they see the final straw, they will drink hell completely. You do not want what they plan, cats. You will never find the evil great enough to achieve what they will find in their heart.

Ensure you silence them quickly if it comes to this. Every last one.

From Ashveil.

The cat is always our only hope.
