The Last Cat: The Early Years

Game Overview

Genre: Dark fantasy 2D Metroidvania action RPG with fast-paced melee combo combat, magic skills, exploration, puzzles, romance, and deep spiritual/redemption themes.

Length: Approximately 35–45 hours for the main story.

Tone: Dark fantasy with moments of wonder, horror, comedy, and profound emotional/spiritual depth. Horror elements are tasteful and psychological, inspired by real spiritual warfare experiences.

Core Loop: Explore interconnected cave systems, solve environmental puzzles (especially water flow), fight enemies and bosses, build relationships with the Six Stars, collect letters and relics, upgrade abilities, and uncover the truth about the sealed astral flow.

Future Vision: This game is the foundation for the larger "Rescue the Stars" persistent MMO universe. Progress, characters, relationships, and certain items carry over.

Controls & Combat System

Basic Controls

Jump: X

Claw Attack: Square (light melee combo starter)

Sword Attack: Triangle (unlocked later)

Roll / Dodge: Circle

Parry: L1 (timed press to stop attacks)

Augmented / Strong Attack: Hold R1 + face button (powerful version of claw or sword)

Ability System

You can equip up to 8 abilities total.

Initially limited to abilities from one active Star.

Later expand to two Stars and four active abilities from them.

Assign abilities with L2/R2 + face buttons.

Abilities include melee combos, projectile beams (Metroid-style), and special skills.

Astral Expression Mode: Triggered when stardust meter is full. Enhances combos, makes them more fluid/musical, adds astral effects, and generates mana while active.

Mana vs Stardust

Mana: Borrowed from the Stars / angel powers. Used for most magic.

Stardust: Earned naturally by the Last Cat (astral conduit) through combat actions. Builds when hitting enemies. Water attacks reduce it.

Astral Abilities: Mana-free versions of skills learned through attunement. These are the cat’s true power.

The cat is an astral conduit — this is why cats hate water (it makes them "leak" stardust).

Combo Depth Example (Elusiaa Holy Path)

Holy projectile ? Claw 1 ? Claw 2 ? Holy Claw ? Claw 2 ? Claw 4 ? Holy Pounce (with halo birds carrying the cat for a giant claw strike). Bonus damage if used after strong combos.

The Six Stars (Angel Bearers / Sages)

Each Star is a powerful magic user tied to an elemental domain. They are children of Benaveil’s forms in different aspects. They can become allies, friends, and romantic interests.

Elusiaa – Light (child of Racha)
Warm, guiding, holy magic. Main early companion and story guide. Blonde, radiant, kind but carries ancient strength.

Khavia – Water (child of Chavera)
Flowing, healing, aquatic powers. Teal/feline features in some designs.

Auerlis – Fire (child of Simchi)
Passionate, destructive flame magic. Fiery orange/red aesthetic.

Maera – Blood (child of Maerrow)
Life force, sacrifice, bone and flesh themes. Darker, crimson accents.

Kaelia – Void (child of Kaeliss)
Mysterious, shadow/void magic, portals. Purple with DNA-like tattoos in astral form. Early romance candidate.

Nyxari – Shadow (child of Nyxara)
Stealth, icy flames, darkness. Platinum hair with icy flame effects.

Star Mechanics:

Each has unique skill trees, melee combo starters, and projectile beam styles.

Visual appearance changes with equipped outfits and when casting.

Relationship building through quests, conversations, letters, and helping settlements.

Girlfriend system (optional/toggleable): Special talents, better rewards, interactive petting scenes (wholesome cat interaction), and unique ending bonuses.

Key Locations & World Structure

Virethorn Section (First Area)

Dark, spooky, rocky. Poisoned water flow puzzles.

Early meetings with Elusiaa and Kaelia.

Introduction to water manipulation mechanics.

In the second section of Virethorn’s domain you will solve a simple water manipulation puzzle and then gain access to the first settlement. It is populated by goblins who torture the slimes and force them to clean water for them.  This and the behavior modification torture the goblins put the slimes through is painful to see and makes you hate the goblins and empathize with the slimes.

Upper Caves

More action-packed, less "evil" darkness.

House-sized slime boss.

Goblin and harpy settlement with conflicts (bridge access, slimes, etc.).

Meet Maera and Khavia.

Lower Caves

High-tech ancient feel with bone/flesh tilesets, skulls in walls.

Bone Bloom boss (massive fleshy bone quadruped with astral core).

Meet Auerlis.

Slime fragment / pet unlock.

Astral Caverns

Sandy areas, closer to the astral sea.

Leviathan awaits.

Settlements with ninjas and strange beings.

Final story revelations and sword unlocks.

Settlement Conflicts: Dynamic events that require quests, conversation choices, or Star intervention. Resolving them builds relationships and affects romance/endings.

Major Bosses

Virethorn: Colossal room-filling poison vine titan with green glowing core, massive purple tentacles, orange spikes, and dripping venom. Multiple refined designs exist.

Slimastadon: House-sized slime titan (astral core version of regular slimes).

Bone Bloom: Fleshy bone quadruped guardian with skull face, massive tail and limbs. Connected to Bahamut themes.

Behemoth forms: Earth guardian titan.

Leviathan: Ancient astral dragon, final major guardian. Multi-stage potential.

Final Battles: Stars (if rewritten path) + multi-stage Benaveil (duel or tragic).

Bosses are epic, long fights (some ~30 minutes) with distinct phases and environmental interaction.

Companions & Pets

Marrowlily: Slime pet (from Bone Bloom astral core). Levels up, gains skills (spit attacks, etc.). Can be possessed temporarily by lesser spirit Dubolily for combat.

Stars: Primary companions when equipped. They are summoned and cast spells or skills when their abilities are used.

Romance & Relationship System

Deep relationship building with the Six Stars.

Influenced by: Conversation choices, quests, settlement resolutions, letter returns, outfit collection, and specific swords (e.g., Aetherfang for Kaelia romance boost).

Girlfriend Mode (optional): Special talent trees, capstone abilities, better rewards, interactive wholesome scenes (cat petting/purring), and unique story moments.

Visual feedback: Stars appear in sexy/powerful outfits when casting. Equipment on Stars provides buffs.

Letters, Lore & Spiritual Themes

Mysterious Letters: Many addressed to Leviathan or Behemoth. Found throughout the world. Returning them advances story, relationships, and unlocks ancient swords/knowledge.

Sage Cat Writings: From ancient sages like Ashveil, Flameveil, Archstride, Sablethorn, etc. Contain wisdom, prophecies, and clues.

Guardian Cherubim Exposition (Job 40–41):

Bahamut / Behemoth: Internal strength, endurance of suffering, protection of marrow (life source), discernment (joint from marrow), ability to "consume" heavy spiritual meat/suffering.

Leviathan: External authority, bridled tongue (holy speech), unbreakable scales of unity, heart like a millstone, power to turn turbulent seas into healing ointment.

Central thesis: Even fallen angels and dark spirits can be redeemed through wisdom, mercy, and testing of character. The game is a loving rebuke and path to restoration.

"Rescue the Stars" has multiple layers: rescuing the Six Stars, rescuing fallen angels in real life metaphor, and ultimately rescuing hearts.

Private / Inspired Sections:

Heavy inspiration from real spiritual warfare experiences (10-year attack by a dark angel named Benaviel / Chaveal / Simchi).

Nightmares, visions, control over thoughts/meds, family oppression, etc., translated into game horror elements (distorted overlays, "Let Us Call Hell", possessions in Hell visits, etc.).

Goal: Teach players how dark forces operate while offering hope and redemption paths.

Endings & Narrative Branches

Unity / Redemption Ending: Duel with Benaveil using astral powers + learned Star abilities. He can be saved and become a true guardian cherub. Triumphant music.

Tragic Ending: Benaveil annihilates the cats or takes his own life in despair. "Let Us Call Hell" plays. Heavy emotional impact.

Sorcerer / Void Path: Align with darker forces, grand theft auto style choices. Leads to powerful but unstable void stream abilities.

Rewrite Path: Benaveil rewrites the game into Mega Man-style boss rush against the Stars + final angel stages. High tension resource management.

Multiple romance outcomes affect final scenes and carry-over potential.

Music & Sound Design

Main Theme: "O Sent (Let Us Go)" – Veiled fight song that can feel threatening or triumphant depending on context.

Dark Moments: Distorted "Let Us Call Hell" – haunting, used during possessions, nightmares, or when Benaveil is enraged.

Unity Ending: Triumphant remix of "O Sent".

Ambient cave themes, boss battle tracks, MIDI/Serum custom sounds for unique atmosphere.

Music can distort during horror sequences.

Additional Systems

Items: Slime Gel (health), Tuna (mana). Limited carry capacity that upgrades.

Jwls: Currency from breaking boxes and enemy drops. Used in settlements.

Hell Visits: Optional dangerous area with simplified black & white fighting game arcade, possessions, cameos, and story. Can redeem fragments of Benaveil.

Astral Books: Save points that record story/progress.

Transformation: Full Astral Dragoth form (dragon + mammoth features) in late game and final battles. Peek at deeper transformation system for sequel.

Community / Future: Art submissions, NFTs as placeholders for community content that builds new bosses/dungeons in the larger universe.

Spiritual & Thematic Core

This game is fundamentally about:

Testing and refining character through suffering (inspired by MLK’s “content of their character”).

Redemption is possible even for the darkest beings if they choose hope.

The astral stream as a manifestation of divine will and love.

Teaching players (through gameplay and story) how to recognize and respond to spiritual darkness with wisdom and mercy rather than fear or hatred.

“Rescue the Stars” = rescuing hearts, including those of fallen angels.

The game is not preachy — it presents these ideas through fun, dark fantasy RPG gameplay, beautiful pixel art, deep lore, and emotional storytelling.
