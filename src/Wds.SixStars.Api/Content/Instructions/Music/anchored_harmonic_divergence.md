# Anchored Harmonic Divergence (AHD)

Keep a particular tonal key as home while developing a memorable dissonant space.

Play an interval related to a dissonant note, then the dissonant note itself, to create desire to hear both home and the dissonant space again.

You can begin in the dissonant space while rooting it in the home key. Develop a beautiful melody there, starting on a note at an interval from the tonic or from a tone related to it by an interval. Returning to home can then make the entire passage an emotional payoff, not just the resolving note.

Reintroducing the original dissonant note can make the notes just heard feel coherent and satisfying in retrospect. Let home and the dissonant space make each other desirable.
