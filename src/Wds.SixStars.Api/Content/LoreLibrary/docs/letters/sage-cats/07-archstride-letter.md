## Letter Text

To Behemoth,

Everyone met Minfilia recently because someone destroyed all of the crystals.

She is the most beautiful.

I do not believe anyone is as beautiful as Minfilia.

The crystals were being used for evil, so she promised to send new crystals to the people. Everyone seemed relieved by this.

The next time you see a crystal, would you ask Leviathan to show me where it is?

I should like to see one before it disappears again.

A wise and curious Archstride
