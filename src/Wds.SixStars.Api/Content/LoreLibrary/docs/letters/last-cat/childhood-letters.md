# Childhood Letters

These are letters the Last Cat brought with him as clues from his life before arriving on the planet. They are not all cave pickups. They can be displayed in the opening, replayed in the Astral Book Room, or used as evidence during AI conversations with the Stars.

| Letter | Use |
|---|---|
| [Mug Letter](01-mug-letter.md) | Opening crawl with smiling mug pixel art; establishes hunger, Dyvena, and impossible kindness. |
| [Concerned Mother](02-concerned-mother.md) | Warns against counterfeit stardust and points toward Leviathan. |
| [Angry Father](03-angry-father.md) | Says the hidden place is nowhere and the Stars know the way. |
| [Student Letter](04-student-letter.md) | Shows the Last Cat becoming a myth among children. |
