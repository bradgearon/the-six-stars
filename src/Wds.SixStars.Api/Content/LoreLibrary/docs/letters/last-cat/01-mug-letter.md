## Letter Text

To Leviathan,

Thank you for the beautiful mug, Dyvena.

The children sat and smiled at it all day. I do not know what they saw in it, but they would not stop passing it around. It would be wonderful if there were many smaller mugs like it.

For a little while, they forgot they were hungry.

~ A dying mother

## Developer Notes

Use the pixel-art smiling mug image during the opening crawl. This is one of the first proofs that the letters know the Last Cat before he understands why.
