# Meeting the Stars

Canonical full meeting scenes are preserved in `docs/source-imports/meeting-the-stars.md`. The six angel-story conversations have also been promoted into the curated file [`star-angel-stories.md`](star-angel-stories.md), because they are central relationship, wisdom, and Benaveil-lore material.

| Star | Scene placement | Angel story | Emotional purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kaelia | Before Virethorn water puzzle | Dark angel made her reflection drown in tar-water | Introduce void, mystery, and hope under attack. |
| Maera | Flesh-and-bones area before Bone Bloom | Dark form filled her veins with worms and bleeding visions | Introduce blood, possession, rage, and building from rot. |
| Auerlis | Entrance to Astral Caverns | Old spirit barely escaped a kind-faced dark angel who weaponized shame | Introduce fire, joy against terror, and false love that consumes. |
| Elusiaa | Early game after helping goblin-harpy settlement | Good angel brought water to a dying valley | Establish mercy, service, and the warm guide role. |
| Khavia | After final bone bridge and Bone Bloom confrontation | Good angel healed enemies in blood-red canyons | Establish friendship, healing after battle, bridge-building, and enemies as hurt/lost. |
| Nyxari | Middle of Astral Caverns | Good angel possessed a dead man, then left him restored as a gift | Establish shadow, fragmentation, complicated hope, and gifts left behind. |

## Production rule

Every Star meeting should do five things:

1. Show the area's visual identity.
2. Reveal the Star's wound and domain.
3. Tell either a dark-angel warning or a good-angel memory.
4. Give the Last Cat a chance to respond with compassion, humor, shrewdness, flirtation, or foolishness.
5. Create a later callback in settlement conflicts, letters, romance, Benaveil's heart, or the final rewrite.

## Angel-story structure

- **Kaelia / Maera / Auerlis:** dark-angel stories that make the game feel frightening and teach what the greater evils do.
- **Elusiaa / Khavia / Nyxari:** good-angel stories that keep hope alive and teach that some angels still serve, heal, and give gifts.

See [`star-angel-stories.md`](star-angel-stories.md) for full text and implementation notes.
