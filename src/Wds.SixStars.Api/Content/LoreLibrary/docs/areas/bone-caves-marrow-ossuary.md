# Bone Caves - Marrow Ossuary

## Purpose

The Bone Caves are the visceral heart of the midgame. The player carries heavy bones, builds bridges over blood/marrow hazards, meets Maera, deepens Khavia's bridge-building theme, and confronts Bone Bloom, a Bahamut/Behemoth-style guardian of life force.

## Visual direction

Use pages 08 and 09. The palette is bone, old red flesh, purple-black shadow, skulls embedded in walls, chains, marrow pools, organic platforms, bone arches, and strange alien flowers growing from rot.

## Main gameplay

- Bone bridge construction.
- Heavy-object carry challenges.
- Blood/marrow environmental hazards.
- Bone mage and undead encounters.
- Plant/flesh/bone hybrid ecology.
- Maera and Khavia moral tension.

## Maera meeting

Maera appears from a ribcage-like alcove while the cat carries bones. She is furious, wounded, and drawn to the cat's strange insistence on building something good from rot.

## Bone Bloom

Bone Bloom is a major boss and future ally/guardian. It is tied to Behemoth/Bahamut themes: internal strength, bones guarding marrow/life force, digesting suffering, and protecting hidden life in the marsh.

Defeating Bone Bloom yields the Slime Fragment/Slime Core that leads to Marrowlily.

## Enemy families

- Undead and bone servants.
- Bone Mage.
- Ossuary Dragon.
- Bone Bloom runts, thorn-squids, choir husks.
- Bloodroot and marrow plant life.
