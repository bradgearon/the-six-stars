# Lower Caves - Virethorn Sanctuary

## Purpose

The Lower Caves are the first major area and the player's first false sanctuary. They introduce cave dread, poisoned water, slimes, goblins, harpies, Elusiaa, Kaelia, Virethorn corruption, and the first emotional decisions that touch Benaveil's heart.

## Visual direction

Use the Ancient Wonder Caves and Virethorn's Lair references. The mood is dark stone, blue and purple crystals, hanging vines, scattered candles, wet rock, corrupted green poison, purple thorns, and ritual-like plant architecture.

## Main gameplay

- Poisoned water flow puzzles.
- First Star ability restriction.
- Slime encounters and moral setup.
- Goblin-harpie settlement as first hub.
- First Sanctuary music.
- Virethorn corruption enemy family.
- Early Kaelia meeting before water puzzle.
- Early Elusiaa guidance after helping settlement.

## Settlement conflict

Goblins torture slimes and force them to clean water. This makes the player hate the goblins and empathize with slimes. Later, the goblin-harpie social dynamic is revealed to be more complicated, including rough play that can be misread as cruelty.

## Key AI scenes

- Elusiaa and the slime gel.
- Elusiaa and the goblin throwing harpies into the river.
- Hidden holy-prank route against the sleeping goblin.
- Kaelia's first conversation about the Void and tar-like despair.
