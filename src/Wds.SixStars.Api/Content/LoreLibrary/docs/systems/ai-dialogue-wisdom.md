# AI Dialogue and Wisdom System

## Purpose

AI conversations are not a side feature. They are how the player befriends the Stars, influences Benaveil, learns wisdom, and turns the game into a living moral and psychological training ground.

The player is not simply picking "good" or "evil" dialogue options. The player often has incomplete information and must read the situation. Wisdom means asking the right question before acting.

## Format

Each major AI scenario should have:

- **Premise:** what the Star believes is happening.
- **Hidden truth:** what is actually happening or what can be discovered.
- **Emotional state:** what the Star wants and fears.
- **Benaveil ripple:** how this touches the angel's heart.
- **Guardrails:** what the AI must not contradict.
- **Outcome variables:** bond, trust, wisdom, shrewdness, kindness, dark-force pressure, unlocks.

## Example: Elusiaa and the goblin-harpies

Elusiaa tells the Last Cat that a goblin dragged three harpies across a bridge and threw them into the river. She is angry and considering casting Holy on him to pay him back. Benaveil's heart is connected because the impulse came from him.

Hidden truth: the goblin is a good person and the harpies were enjoying the rough play. Earlier lore or observation can reveal that harpies often smile when they play rough.

Possible player approaches:

- encourage stronger punishment;
- tell her to stop and investigate;
- ask how the harpies reacted;
- calm her anger without denying her pain;
- turn it into a prank route;
- use the situation to teach mercy and discernment.

## Hidden comedy route: three holy prank nights

If the player and Elusiaa choose the playful route, they may cast a harmless holy spell on the goblin while he sleeps for three nights. Each night escalates the gag. The goblin wakes up confused, suspicious, then prepared. The route should be funny and memorable, not cruel.

This route supports the Kind/Fun ending style: the game can be dark and still have laughter.

## Example: slime gel tasting

The player may reason that violent slimes drop gel and perhaps the gel is useful or edible. Elusiaa can react with concern, curiosity, disgust, mercy, or humor depending on the player's framing. This scenario tests whether the player objectifies slimes, studies them respectfully, or learns that even enemies may be victims of corruption.

## Audio feedback

- Negative/carnal choices: distorted "Let Us Call Hell" motifs.
- Wise/hopeful choices: cleaner "O Sent" or "Let Us Go" motifs.
- Ambiguous/funny choices: mixed cue, perhaps unsettling but playful.

## State variables

Suggested variables:

- `star_bond_[name]`
- `star_trust_[name]`
- `benaveil_hope`
- `benaveil_lawlessness_pressure`
- `wise_score`
- `shrewd_score`
- `kind_fun_score`
- `dark_force_frequency`
- `romance_interest_[name]`
- `settlement_reputation_[settlement]`
