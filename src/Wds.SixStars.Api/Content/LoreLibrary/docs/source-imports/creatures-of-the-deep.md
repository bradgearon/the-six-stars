## 1. Lumenmaw Crawlers

Elements: Light + Blood
Type: Alien cave predator / jaw-beast
A pale, almost holy-looking creature with a split glowing skull-mouth and red veins under translucent skin. It crawls on four hooked limbs and “prays” before attacking.

How it fights:
It blinds the player with short light flashes, then lunges with a blood-draining bite. When wounded, its glow turns red and it gets faster.

Why it works:
It feels like corrupted sacred life. Very good early-mid enemy for the Fissures of Hidden Light.

## 2. Voidbat Choir

Elements: Void + Shadow
Type: Bat swarm / sound enemy
A cluster of long-winged alien bats with empty black faces and glowing ring-mouths. They hang upside down like a living chandelier.

How it fights:
They scream in waves that distort the screen and reverse gravity for a second. Some dive, some stay back and chant.

Why it works:
Very alien, very gamey, and perfect for rooms where falling is dangerous.

## 3. Ashscale Slime Dragons

Elements: Fire + Water + Slime
Type: Small dragon-slime hybrid
A crawling salamander-dragon made of boiling slime, with obsidian scales floating inside its body. Its face melts and reforms constantly.

How it fights:
It spits steam blobs that linger as hazards. If hit with fire, it hardens into armor. If hit with water, it splits into two smaller slimes.

Why it works:
Great because the player has to think instead of just attacking.

## 4. Oculthorax Apostles

Elements: Light + Shadow + Void
Type: Eye-thorax alien knight
A tall insectoid enemy with no head. Its chest is one giant eye surrounded by smaller rotating eyes. Its arms are long bone-lances.

How it fights:
The big eye charges a beam, while the small eyes track the player and fire tiny seeking bolts. When the player gets close, it stabs with its thorax-lances.

Why it works:
This could become an iconic enemy family. It is alien, readable, and threatening.

## 5. Marrowreef Goblins

Elements: Bone Bloom + Water + Blood
Type: Goblin / reef parasite
Little cave goblins wearing living coral-bone armor grown from Bone Bloom fragments. They look silly at first, but their armor is alive.

How it fights:
They throw bone hooks, hide in walls, and summon small blood-coral spikes from the floor. When killed, the armor crawls away and tries to attach to another goblin.

Why it works:
Good mix of creepy and mischievous. They give the world culture and ecology, not just monsters.

## 6. Leviathan’s Tonguefish

Elements: Water + Shadow
Type: Floating eel / tongue creature
