# The Six Stars

The Six Stars are the early focus of The Last Cat: The Early Years. They are powerful sages and spell-bearers tied to Benaveil's forms. They are flawed, beautiful, emotionally complex, and central to combat, AI conversation, romance, and the rewrite finale.

| Star | Element | Daughter/Form | Domain | Core Quote | Magical Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Elusiaa](elusiaa.md) | Light | Daughter of Racha | Mercy | “Mercy is how we remember we’re not gods.” | Lumen’s Grace staff |
| [Khavia](khavia.md) | Water | Daughter of Chavera | Friendship | “Somebody helped me once.” | Tideweaver’s Codex |
| [Auerlis](auerlis.md) | Fire | Daughter of Simchi | Joy | “Then at least they are laughing.” | Ember Eternal lantern |
| [Maera](maera.md) | Blood | Daughter of Maerrow | Possession | “If I can’t have you, no one can.” | Sanguine Heart crystal |
| [Kaelia](kaelia.md) | Void | Daughter of Kaeliss | Mystery | “The Void is not empty. It is listening.” | Nullheart Orb |
| [Nyxari](nyxari.md) | Shadow | Daughter of Nyxara | Fragmentation | “If nothing matters, nothing can break.” | Eclipse Mirror |



## Angel story pairing

The six Stars should each carry one important angel story. These stories are now curated in [`../../dialogue/star-angel-stories.md`](../../dialogue/star-angel-stories.md).

| Pair | Stars | Narrative job |
|---|---|---|
| Dark warnings | Kaelia, Maera, Auerlis | Show what dark angels do to hope, the body, memory, and meaning. |
| Good memories | Elusiaa, Khavia, Nyxari | Show that angels can still serve, heal, and leave gifts behind. |

These stories are major relationship anchors and should be available as later AI-dialogue callbacks.
